# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and take constructive feedback
gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, personal or political
attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that do not follow this code, and may ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

To report a problem, e-mail the maintainers (the corresponding authors listed in the
[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04557)). Every report is reviewed and kept confidential.
